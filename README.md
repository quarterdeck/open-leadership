# Open Leadership

Open Leadership (OL) is an open framework of qualifications and profiling tools for managers,
leaders and people professionals. Anyone can read it, use it and adapt it at no cost.

Quarterdeck, a leadership and management development company in Harrogate, UK, commissions and
maintains the framework.

## What the framework will contain

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| OL Management Qualifications | Management practice, from first-line manager to strategic level |
| OL Leadership Qualifications | Leadership practice, with a coaching and mentoring pathway |
| OL People Practice Qualifications | HR and L&D practice, with a recognition scheme for practitioners |
| OL Behaviour Profile | A questionnaire on behavioural style at work |
| OL Preference Profile | A questionnaire on working preferences |

All qualifications use one bank of units and one credit system.

## Independent and unregulated

Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. It is not regulated by Ofqual, and no
awarding organisation or professional body accredits or endorses it. All content is independently
written from public facts and open sources. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).

The profiles are for development and self-awareness. They are not validated for recruitment or
selection decisions.

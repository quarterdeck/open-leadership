# How Open Leadership compares

Version 0.1.0. Kris approved the publication of this page on 19 November 2026. Facts about other organisations were taken from their public pages and
from the Ofqual register in November 2026. They can change. The sources are in
[SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) and [research/source-notes.md](../research/source-notes.md).

## Read this first

Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. It is not regulated by Ofqual. No
awarding organisation, professional body or test publisher accredits, endorses, approves or
recognises it. It has no connection with any organisation that this page names. An Open
Leadership qualification is not the same thing as a regulated qualification, and it does not
replace one where an employer, a regulator or a funding rule asks for one.

"Comparable in level and scope" on this page means two things only: the qualification is
written to the public level descriptors of the same level, and it covers a similar range of
subjects. It says nothing about recognition, quality or value.

All trade marks belong to their owners. This page names them only to identify them.

## What is different

| | Open Leadership | Regulated qualifications from an awarding organisation |
|---|---|---|
| Regulation | None | Ofqual, or the regulator of another UK nation |
| Who can deliver and assess | Any organisation, with no approval | A centre that the awarding organisation approved |
| Who checks the assessment | A second person checks a sample of the decisions, and a panel reviews each Certificate or Diploma | The centre, then the awarding organisation |
| Who issues the certificate | The organisation that assessed the learner | The awarding organisation |
| Content | Open. Each unit, guide and handbook is public under the MIT licence | Copyright of the awarding organisation |
| Cost of the framework | None | Centre and learner fees |
| Recognition by employers | Not known. The framework is new | Established |
| Public funding and apprenticeships | Not eligible | Often eligible |
| Membership of a professional body | None | Often linked to a qualification |

## Management and leadership

The Chartered Management Institute (CMI), and ILM (a brand of City & Guilds), offer regulated
management and leadership qualifications from Level 2 to Level 7 or 8, in the sizes Award,
Certificate and Diploma. Open Leadership has qualifications at Levels 3, 5 and 7 only.

| Level | Typical learner | Open Leadership (credits) | Public minimum sizes of CMI's main qualification at the level (credits) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Team leader, first-line manager | Award 10, Certificate 25 | Award 5, Certificate 13, Diploma 37 |
| 5 | Middle manager | Certificate 18, Diploma 42 | Award 4, Certificate 13, Diploma 37 |
| 7 | Senior manager, senior leader | Certificate 20, Diploma 40 | Award 7, Certificate 14, Diploma 37 |

- One credit is 10 hours of total qualification time in both systems.
- Open Leadership has no Level 3 Diploma, no Level 2, 4, 6 or 8, and no Extended Diploma.
- Both use units, work-based assignments and other work evidence, criteria that the learner
  must all meet, and a result of pass or refer. Neither uses examinations.
- Open Leadership units have one fixed size at each level (5, 6 and 10 credits). The regulated
  qualifications have units of different sizes and a wider choice of units.
- Open Leadership adds a panel review before a Certificate or a Diploma is issued. At Level 7
  one member of the panel is from outside the learner's organisation.
- Open Leadership has a coaching and mentoring family at Levels 3, 5 and 7. Both of the
  organisations above have such a family also.
- CMI gives membership grades and a chartered status. Open Leadership has no membership and
  no chartered status.

## People practice

The Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD) offers regulated qualifications
for HR and learning and development practitioners at Levels 3, 5 and 7, and membership grades.

| Level | Open Leadership | CIPD (from the Ofqual register) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Certificate, 25 credits, 5 units | Foundation certificate, 24 credits, 4 units |
| 5 | Diploma, 42 credits, 7 units, two pathways | Associate diploma, 42 credits, 7 units, two diplomas |
| 7 | Diploma, 60 credits, 6 units | Advanced diploma, 120 credits, 8 units, two diplomas |

- **The Level 7 diploma of Open Leadership is half the size.** It is comparable in level. It
  is not comparable in size.
- Both use scenario-based or work-based assignments and no examination. The CIPD Level 7
  assessments have grades of pass, merit and distinction. Open Leadership has pass or refer
  only.
- CIPD membership grades, which include chartered grades, come from a qualification or from
  an assessment of experience. Many UK employers ask for them. The Open Leadership recognition
  scheme has three grades that an organisation confirms for itself. It is a first design, it
  gives no membership, and no employer is known to ask for it.
- The CIPD Profession Map is copyright. Open Leadership does not use it. The Open Leadership
  competence framework was written from open government sources.

## The two profiles

| | OL Behaviour Profile | OL Preference Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | To describe how a person says that they act with people and tasks at work | To describe the conditions in which a person does their best work |
| Commercial tools with a similar purpose | DiSC and Everything DiSC (John Wiley & Sons) | Insights Discovery (The Insights Group) |
| Result | Four scores from 0 to 100, each a mark and a band on a line | Five scores from 0 to 100, each between two named sides. One scale is optional |
| Types, letters, colours, wheel | None | None |
| Items | 48, from the public-domain International Personality Item Pool | 60, from the same public source |
| Evidence | **None yet.** No pilot, no norms, no reliability or validity data | **None yet** |
| Cost | None | None |
| Training or licence to use it | None is required. A facilitator guide is public | None is required |

- The commercial tools have published research, norm groups, trained practitioners, software
  and support. The Open Leadership profiles have none of these yet.
- The commercial tools give a person a style or a colour type. The Open Leadership profiles
  do not, by design: a result is a position on each line, with its uncertainty.
- The Open Leadership profiles use no items, scoring, report text, names or graphics of any
  commercial tool. Their items and scoring are public, so anybody can check them.
- Like the commercial tools named here, the Open Leadership profiles are for development.
  They must not be used for recruitment or selection.

## When to choose which

Choose a regulated qualification or an established commercial tool if you need recognition
outside your organisation, public funding, professional membership, or a tool with evidence
behind it today.

Open Leadership can fit if you want a structure for in-house development at a known level,
content that you can read and change before you start, assessment by your own people with a
panel review, and no fees for the framework.

# Credit and assessment rules

Version 0.1.0 (draft for approval).

These rules apply to every Open Leadership unit and qualification. Open Leadership is an
independent, unregulated framework. Any organisation can deliver, assess and certify against
these rules, at no cost and with no permission.

Section 6 (who can issue a certificate) is a first design. It is open for change.

## 1. Credit and time

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total qualification time (TQT) | The estimated time that a typical learner needs: guided learning, private study, practice at work and assessment |
| Guided learning hours (GLH) | The part of TQT with a tutor, coach or facilitator present, in a room or online |
| Credit | 1 credit is 10 hours of TQT |

The guide share of guided learning is 40% at Level 3, 35% at Level 5 and 25% at Level 7. It is a
guide. A centre can deliver a unit with more or less guided learning if the learner can still
meet the outcomes.

## 2. Units

| Level | Unit size | TQT | Guide GLH |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 credits | 50 hours | 20 hours |
| 5 | 6 credits | 60 hours | 21 hours |
| 7 | 10 credits | 100 hours | 25 hours |

- All units at one level have the same size.
- A unit code has the level, the domain and a number: `OL5-PEO-02`.
- A unit can be taken alone. The learner gets a unit record, and the credit counts towards a
  later qualification.
- Each unit has a purpose, learning outcomes, assessment criteria, a workplace scenario and
  evidence requirements.

## 3. Qualification sizes

| Size | TQT | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Award | 120 hours or less | 12 or less |
| Certificate | 121 to 369 hours | 13 to 36 |
| Diploma | 370 hours or more | 37 or more |

These are the size thresholds that Ofqual publishes for regulated qualifications (Ofqual
Handbook, Crown copyright, used under the Open Government Licence v3.0). Open
Leadership uses the same thresholds so that the words mean what UK readers expect.

Titles follow the pattern `OL Level [n] [Award | Certificate | Diploma] in [Subject]`.
In running text, write "Open Leadership" in full.

## 4. Rules of combination

- Each qualification specification lists its core units, its pathway units and its optional
  units.
- All units are at the level of the qualification in this version.
- A unit counts towards more than one qualification. A learner who holds one qualification
  needs only the units that they do not have for the next one at the same level.
- Recognition of prior learning: a centre can accept other evidence of learning for up to half
  of the credit of a qualification. The centre records what it accepted and why.
- Entry: there are no formal entry requirements. The learner must have access to work that can
  give evidence at the scope of the level.

### Decision: the size of the Level 7 people practice diploma

The OL Level 7 Diploma in Strategic People Practice is **60 credits** (6 units, 600 hours).

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| 60 credits | A working practitioner can complete it in one to two years. It fits the standard Level 7 unit. It keeps cost and time low, which is the purpose of Open Leadership. It is larger than the other Level 7 diplomas (40 credits), which reflects the specialist content | Some employers will see it as lighter than a larger qualification |
| 120 credits | Some employers expect a larger qualification at this level | 1,200 hours is a barrier for most people in work. It needs 12 Level 7 units, and the bank would need 6 more specialist units of lower value |

The decision is 60 credits. If employers ask for a larger award, a later version can add an
extended diploma from the same units.

## 5. Assessment

### Principles

- **Evidence from real work.** The learner shows each outcome with evidence from their own
  work. All Open Leadership assessment material is public, so no assessment depends on a secret
  question.
- **Criterion based.** The learner must meet each assessment criterion of a unit.
- **No examinations.**

### Methods

Work-based assignment, portfolio of work evidence, observation at work, witness statement,
professional discussion, reflective account, project report, presentation. The unit says which
methods fit. The centre can select from them.

### Results

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pass | The evidence meets every criterion |
| Refer | One or more criteria are not met. The assessor says which, and the learner submits more evidence |

There are no grades above pass in this version. A qualification is passed when all of its units
are passed and the panel review (section 6) is passed.

### Authenticity

- The learner signs a statement that the evidence is their own work.
- A person at work who saw the work (not the assessor) confirms the main evidence.
- If the learner used an AI tool, they say where and how. The panel review tests that the
  learner understands and owns the work.

## 6. Who can issue a certificate

Any organisation can issue an Open Leadership certificate. Nobody needs approval from
Quarterdeck or from any other body. A certificate is valid if the issuing organisation followed
the process below and keeps the record that proves it.

### Step 1: unit assessment

An assessor judges the evidence for each unit against the criteria.

- The assessor knows the subject and holds, or works at, the level of the unit or above.
- The assessor is not the learner's line manager and has no personal interest in the result.
- A second person checks a sample of the assessor's decisions.

### Step 2: panel review

For a Certificate or a Diploma, the learner then presents their work to a panel. The model is
the oral examination of a research degree: the learner explains what they did, why, and what
they learned, and the panel questions them.

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Panel size | Three people for a Diploma. Two for a Certificate |
| Chair | A person who has not taught or assessed the learner |
| Independent member | At least one member from outside the learner's team. At Level 7, at least one member from outside the learner's organisation |
| Subject member | At least one member who practises at the level of the qualification or above |
| Length | About 30 minutes at Level 3, 45 minutes at Level 5, 60 minutes at Level 7 |
| Content | A short presentation by the learner, then questions on the evidence, on the decisions the learner made, and on what they would do differently |
| Decision | Pass or refer, by agreement of the panel, with written reasons. A referred learner can return to a panel one time with the same members or with new members |

An Award has no panel. The unit assessment and the second check are sufficient.

### Step 3: the record

The issuing organisation keeps an assessment record for each certificate for six years from
the date of the certificate, and then deletes it. Six years is the usual time limit for a
claim about a contract in England and Wales, so a record is available while a dispute is
possible. If a learner asks in writing, the organisation can keep the record longer, so that
it can confirm the certificate to an employer. The record holds:

- the learner's name, the qualification title and the version of the specification
- the units, with the assessor's decision and date for each
- an index of the evidence (the evidence itself can stay with the learner or the employer)
- the names and roles of the panel members, and a statement from each that they are independent
  as the rules require
- the panel's decision, reasons and date

The learner gets a copy. With the learner's permission, the organisation shows the record to a
person who asks to verify the certificate.

### The certificate

A certificate states:

- the name of the issuing organisation
- the learner's name, the full qualification title, the version of the specification and the date
- this sentence: "Issued by [organisation] against the Open Leadership specification. Open
  Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework."

A certificate must not say or imply that the qualification is accredited, regulated, endorsed or
approved by a regulator, an awarding organisation or a professional body.

## 7. Badges

The badge files are in [`badges/`](../badges/). They are free to use under these rules.

| Badge | Who can use it |
|---|---|
| Qualification badge (level and size) | A learner who holds a valid certificate, and the organisation that issued it, with reference to that learner or that programme |
| Open Leadership CPD badge | See section 8 |

- Each badge carries the words "independent, unregulated". Do not change the words on a badge. Do not add words such as "accredited" or "approved".
- A training provider can state that a programme "leads to" or "is assessed against" an Open
  Leadership qualification, and show the badge next to that statement.

## 8. Open Leadership CPD

Continuing professional development (CPD) is learning that does not lead to a qualification.

A learning activity or programme can carry the Open Leadership CPD badge if the provider
publishes, next to the badge:

1. the Open Leadership units that the content relates to
2. the number of learning hours
3. the learning objectives

One hour of learning is one CPD hour. The learner keeps their own record of hours. Nobody
approves the activity. The provider is responsible for the accuracy of what it publishes.

CPD hours can later support a claim for recognition of prior learning (section 4), if the
learner also gives evidence against the unit criteria.

## 9. Versions

Each unit, qualification and rule document has a version number with three parts.

| Part | Changes when |
|---|---|
| Major | The change alters what a learner must do or show |
| Minor | The change adds content or makes a requirement clearer |
| Patch | The change corrects an error |

A learner completes against the version that was current when they started, or a later one. A
certificate states the version.

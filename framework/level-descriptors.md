# Level descriptors

Version 0.1.0 (draft for approval).

A level descriptor states the demand of a level. Each Open Leadership unit and qualification has
one level. The author of a unit chooses the level that fits best. A perfect fit is not necessary.

Open Leadership has three levels in this version: 3, 5 and 7. The numbers follow the levels of
the Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF) in England, so that readers can judge the demand of
a qualification. Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. Its qualifications
are not on the RQF.

Each descriptor has three strands:

| Strand | Question |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | What does the learner know and understand? |
| Skills | What can the learner do? |
| Scope | What is the learner responsible for at work? |

The knowledge and skills strands are our own wording, written with reference to the Ofqual level
descriptors. The scope strand is an addition by Open Leadership.

## Level 3

Typical learner: a team leader, a supervisor or a new first-line manager.
Comparable level of demand: A level.

**Knowledge.** The learner:

- knows the facts, procedures and main ideas that apply to their area of work
- understands why the procedures exist and what happens when they are not followed
- knows where to find reliable information and whom to ask

**Skills.** The learner can:

- plan and complete tasks that are defined but not routine
- select a suitable method from the methods that they know
- find and use information to solve a problem
- review how well a method worked and state what to change next time

**Scope.** The learner:

- is responsible for their own work and for the daily work of a small team or a set of tasks
- works within plans, budgets and rules that other people set
- escalates a problem that is outside their authority

## Level 5

Typical learner: a middle manager or an experienced first-line manager.
Comparable level of demand: a foundation degree.

**Knowledge.** The learner:

- has broad knowledge of management practice and of how their organisation works
- understands the main theories and models in a subject, and their limits
- knows that different people see a situation differently, and why

**Skills.** The learner can:

- analyse a problem that is only broadly defined and decide how to deal with it
- collect and interpret evidence, which includes data, to support a decision
- plan the work of other people and adjust the plan when conditions change
- evaluate results against objectives and explain the reasons for the result

**Scope.** The learner:

- is responsible for the results of a team, a function or a project
- manages people, a budget or other resources, and sets objectives for others
- makes decisions within a strategy that other people set, and contributes to that strategy

## Level 7

Typical learner: a senior manager or a director.
Comparable level of demand: a master's degree.

**Knowledge.** The learner:

- has advanced knowledge of management and leadership, and uses it critically
- understands how the parts of an organisation and its environment affect each other
- knows the present debates in a subject and can judge the strength of the evidence

**Skills.** The learner can:

- deal with complex problems in which many factors interact and information is incomplete
- evaluate theory, research and practice, and form an independent view
- design and carry out an investigation that informs a strategic decision
- judge the short-term and long-term effects of a decision on people, money and reputation

**Scope.** The learner:

- is responsible for the direction and results of an organisation or a large part of one
- sets strategy, culture and standards, and is accountable for them to a board, to owners or to
  the public
- leads other leaders

## How to use the descriptors

- A unit author uses the descriptor to set the demand of the learning outcomes and the
  assessment criteria.
- An assessor uses the descriptor to judge borderline evidence. Evidence at Level 5 shows
  analysis and evaluation. A description of events with no analysis is Level 3 evidence.
- A learner does not need a job at the stated scope to start. The learner does need access to
  work that can give evidence at that scope.

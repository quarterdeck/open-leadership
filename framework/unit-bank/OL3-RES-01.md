Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. The level and the credits of this unit are
its own. The unit is not on the Regulated Qualifications Framework.

## Purpose

This unit enables the learner to work within a budget and use time, equipment and materials well.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

The learner must meet each criterion.

| Learning outcome | Assessment criteria |
|---|---|
| 1. Explain how a budget controls the spending of their area | 1.1 Describe the budget that applies to their area: its period, its main lines and its owner<br>1.2 Explain the difference between costs that are fixed and costs that change with the volume of work<br>1.3 Explain the limits of their authority to spend and the procedure to buy goods or services |
| 2. Use resources within a budget | 2.1 Plan the staff time, equipment and materials for a period of work<br>2.2 Use records to monitor spending or usage against the budget<br>2.3 Identify a difference between the budget and the actual figure, and explain its cause to their manager<br>2.4 Apply the organisation's procedures for the storage, care and security of equipment and materials |
| 3. Review the use of resources in their area | 3.1 Identify waste of time, materials or energy in the work of the team<br>3.2 Carry out a change that reduces waste or cost with no loss of quality or safety<br>3.3 Review how they use resources and state what to change next time |

## Indicative content

This content is a guide for the centre. It is not a list of things to assess.

**Outcome 1.** The purpose of a budget: a plan in money, a limit and a means of control. The
budget year and its periods. Common lines for a team: pay, overtime, agency staff, materials,
equipment, repairs, travel. Fixed costs such as rent and salaries. Costs that rise with volume,
such as materials and overtime. The budget holder and the part that the team leader plays.
Orders, approvals, receipts and invoices. The reason that one person does not order, approve
and receive the same goods.

**Outcome 2.** An estimate of the hours, equipment and materials for a job, from past records.
The cost of an hour of staff time. Stock levels: too much stock and too little stock. Records
of usage, timesheets and the monthly budget report. Differences between the budget and the
actual figure, good and bad. Causes: price, volume, timing, error, waste. Checks of equipment,
reports of faults, maintenance, safe storage and stock counts. Health and safety law in general
terms, as it applies to equipment.

**Outcome 3.** Where to look for waste: waiting, rework, damaged stock, items past their date,
lights and machines left on, journeys that are not necessary. Ideas from the team. A saving in
one place that causes a cost in another. A simple count before and after a change. A cheap
option that costs more over time. The environmental effect of the resources that the team uses.

## Workplace scenario

Use this scenario for discussion, or for assessment if the learner cannot use a case from their
own work.

Tomasz leads the timber and building materials department of a large DIY store, with a team of
six. Halfway through the year, the department has used 70% of its overtime budget. Offcuts and
damaged sheets of board fill a skip each week. The saw that cuts timber for customers has
broken down three times, and each repair came from the store's repairs budget. The store
manager has told all departments to cut costs by 5%. Customers like the cutting service, and it
brings trade customers into the store.

Tasks:

1. List the records that Tomasz needs. Explain what each one can tell him.
2. Identify two causes of waste. Propose a change for each one.
3. Explain what Tomasz can decide and what he must ask the store manager to decide.

## Assessment

Suitable methods: work-based assignment, portfolio of work evidence, professional discussion,
witness statement, observation at work.

Evidence requirements:

- The evidence comes from the real budget or resource records of the learner's area, over a
  period of at least two months.
- A learner who does not hold a budget can use records of the staff hours, materials or
  equipment that they control.
- It includes one plan of resources, one report of a difference to the learner's manager, and
  one change that reduced waste or cost, with figures from before and after.
- Pay details of real people are removed.

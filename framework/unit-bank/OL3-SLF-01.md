## Purpose

This unit enables the learner to manage their own time, priorities and conduct, and plan their development as a manager.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

The learner must meet each criterion.

| Learning outcome | Assessment criteria |
|---|---|
| 1. Plan their own time and priorities to meet the needs of the team | 1.1 Identify the demands on their time and the tasks that matter most to the results of the team<br>1.2 Plan a working week that protects time for priority tasks and for team members<br>1.3 Use a method to deal with interruptions and with tasks that arrive without notice<br>1.4 Review how they used their time against the plan and state what to change |
| 2. Apply the standards of conduct that the organisation expects of a manager | 2.1 Describe the standards of conduct and the rules that apply to their role<br>2.2 Explain how their own behaviour affects the trust and the performance of the team<br>2.3 Identify the signs of pressure in themselves and use a method to manage it |
| 3. Plan and review their own development as a manager | 3.1 Identify their strengths and development needs, with feedback from other people<br>3.2 Plan their development with objectives, activities, dates and measures<br>3.3 Carry out a development activity and review its effect on their work |

## Indicative content

This content is a guide for the centre. It is not a list of things to assess.

**Outcome 1.** The difference between the work of a team member and the work of a team leader.
Urgent tasks and important tasks. A time log as a source of facts. Weekly and daily plans. Time
for people: briefings, one-to-one meetings, time on the floor. Tasks to delegate and tasks to
keep. Ways to say no, or not now. Batches of similar tasks. Limits on email and messages. What
to escalate when the workload is more than the time available.

**Outcome 2.** Sources of standards: the contract, the code of conduct, policies, the values of
the organisation, the manager's own line manager. Reliability, honesty, fairness and
confidentiality in daily work. The change in relationships when a person starts to lead former
peers. Friendship and favouritism. Conduct on social media and outside work. Signs of pressure
in the body, in mood and in behaviour. Simple methods: breaks, boundaries on hours, a
conversation with the line manager, the support that the organisation offers.

**Outcome 3.** Sources of feedback: the line manager, team members, peers, customers, results.
A simple review of strengths and needs against the role. Objectives that are specific and have
a date. Development activities: new tasks, shadowing, a mentor, short courses, reading, practice
with feedback. A development record. How to tell if an activity changed the work.

## Workplace scenario

Use this scenario for discussion, or for assessment if the learner cannot use a case from their
own work.

Callum became team leader of eight sorters at a parcel depot six weeks ago. He was a sorter on
the same shift for four years. When the belt gets busy he goes back to sorting, so his rotas and
safety checks are late and he finishes them at home. A close friend on the team has asked him
twice for the best shifts, and other sorters have noticed. Callum's manager has asked him for a
development plan by the end of the month. Callum says that he has no time to write one.

Tasks:

1. Make a plan for Callum's week that deals with the late rotas and safety checks.
2. Explain how Callum can answer his friend, and why it matters to the team.
3. Write two development objectives for Callum, each with an activity, a date and a measure.

## Assessment

Suitable methods: work-based assignment, portfolio of work evidence, reflective account,
witness statement, professional discussion.

Evidence requirements:

- The evidence comes from the learner's own work over a period of at least four weeks.
- It includes a time log or a weekly plan that the learner used, with a review of the result.
- It includes a development plan and a record of at least one completed activity, with its
  effect on the learner's work.
- Feedback from other people is included with their agreement. Their names are removed.

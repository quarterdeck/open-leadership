Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. The level and the credits of this unit are
its own. The unit is not on the Regulated Qualifications Framework.

## Purpose

This unit enables the learner to manage the performance of a team and develop the capability of its members.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

The learner must meet each criterion.

| Learning outcome | Assessment criteria |
|---|---|
| 1. Set objectives and standards that connect the work of a team to the aims of the organisation | 1.1 Explain how the objectives of the team support the aims of the organisation<br>1.2 Agree objectives with team members that are specific, measurable and achievable<br>1.3 Select measures that show the quality of the work as well as the quantity |
| 2. Review performance and give feedback that changes it | 2.1 Collect evidence of performance from more than one source<br>2.2 Give feedback that describes the work, its effect and the change that is necessary<br>2.3 Analyse the causes of a performance problem before selecting a response<br>2.4 Apply the organisation's procedure to a case of performance that stays below the standard |
| 3. Develop the capability of team members | 3.1 Assess the development needs of the team against present and future work<br>3.2 Plan development that uses work itself, other people and formal learning<br>3.3 Evaluate the effect of a development activity on the performance of a person or the team |
| 4. Evaluate their own practice in the management of performance | 4.1 Evaluate how fair and consistent their decisions about performance are<br>4.2 Plan changes to their practice, with reasons |

## Indicative content

This content is a guide for the centre. It is not a list of things to assess.

**Outcome 1.** The line from organisational aims to team and individual objectives. Objectives
for results and objectives for behaviour. Measures of quantity, quality, time and cost. The risk
that a measure changes behaviour in a way that nobody intended. Agreement of objectives as a
conversation, compared with objectives that are only issued.

**Outcome 2.** Sources of evidence: results, observation, the views of colleagues and customers,
the view of the person. Regular short reviews compared with one annual review. Feedback that is
specific, prompt and about the work. Possible causes of low performance: unclear expectations,
skill, resources, health, the design of the job, motivation, the manager. Informal and formal
stages of a capability procedure. The duty to make reasonable adjustments. Records, and who can
see them.

**Outcome 3.** Needs at the level of the person, the team and the organisation. Learning from
the work: new tasks, projects, shadowing, delegation. Learning from other people: coaching,
mentoring, feedback. Formal learning: courses, qualifications, self-study. How to measure a
change in performance after an activity. Succession and the loss of essential skills.

**Outcome 4.** Common errors of judgement: recent events count for too much, one strong point
colours the whole view, people who are like the manager get higher ratings. Equal treatment of
team members who work different hours or in different places. Sources of feedback on the
manager's own practice.

## Workplace scenario

Use this scenario for discussion, or for assessment if the learner cannot use a case from their
own work.

Farah manages a customer service team of nine people for a housing association. The team answers
on time, but complaints that are reopened have doubled in six months. Two advisers close cases
much faster than the others, and the team's bonus depends on the number of cases closed. One
experienced adviser, who trained most of the team, has become slow and quiet since the new case
system started. Farah has a review meeting with each adviser next month and no budget for
courses this year.

Tasks:

1. Propose objectives and measures for the team that deal with the reopened complaints.
2. Plan the conversation with the experienced adviser. State the evidence that Farah needs first.
3. Make a development plan for the team that needs no course budget.

## Assessment

Suitable methods: work-based assignment, portfolio of work evidence, professional discussion,
witness statement, reflective account.

Evidence requirements:

- The evidence comes from the learner's own management of at least two people over a period of
  at least three months.
- It includes objectives that the learner agreed, a record or account of feedback that the
  learner gave, and a development plan with a review of its effect.
- Records of real people are made anonymous.
- For criterion 2.4, if no case occurred in the period, the learner explains with reference to
  the organisation's procedure how they would act in the scenario.

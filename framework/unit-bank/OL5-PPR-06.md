its own. The unit is not on the Regulated Qualifications Framework.

## Purpose

This unit enables the learner to support learning that occurs during work, between colleagues and from experience.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

The learner must meet each criterion.

| Learning outcome | Assessment criteria |
|---|---|
| 1. Analyse how people learn during work in a part of the organisation | 1.1 Analyse how the people in a team or function learn from tasks, from colleagues and from mistakes<br>1.2 Assess the conditions that help or stop this learning<br>1.3 Select a performance problem that support at the point of work can solve, and justify the selection |
| 2. Plan and apply support for learning during work and between colleagues | 2.1 Plan resources that a person can find and use during a task<br>2.2 Apply a method that helps colleagues to learn from each other<br>2.3 Plan with line managers what they will do to support learning in daily work |
| 3. Apply methods that turn experience into learning | 3.1 Apply a structured review after a piece of work or an incident<br>3.2 Plan work assignments that develop a person or a team<br>3.3 Assess how lessons from one team reach other teams |
| 4. Evaluate the effect of the support and their own practice | 4.1 Evaluate the effect of the support on performance, with evidence from the work<br>4.2 Evaluate their own practice and plan changes, with reasons |

## Indicative content

This content is a guide for the centre. It is not a list of things to assess.

**Outcome 1.** Most learning at work occurs without a course. Sources: the task, questions to
a colleague, observation, feedback, search, mistakes. Methods to find out how people learn now:
observation, interviews, the questions people ask most, search and help-desk records. Conditions:
time, workload, the behaviour of the manager, safety to ask and to admit a mistake, access to
systems, shift patterns, remote work. Problems that suit support at the point of work: tasks done
rarely, tasks with many steps, information that changes often. Problems that need practice away
from the work.

**Outcome 2.** Resources for use during a task: checklists, short guides, worked examples, short
videos, searchable answers, prompts inside a system. Tests of a resource: can a person find it,
is it current, who owns it. Learning between colleagues: buddy arrangements, shadowing, peer
review of work, groups of people who do the same job, short teaching slots in team meetings.
The line manager's part: questions after a task, delegation with support, time for learning. The
role of the learning practitioner changes from provider to enabler.

**Outcome 3.** Review after work: what was planned, what occurred, why, what to do next time.
Review of incidents and near misses without blame. Assignments that develop: a new task, a
project, cover for a colleague, a move between teams. The right level of stretch and support.
Reflection as a habit: notes, short conversations. How lessons spread or stay local: records,
stories, people who move between teams.

**Outcome 4.** Evidence from the work: errors, time to competence, rework, queries to experts,
quality checks, use of resources. The difficulty of showing cause. Views of users and managers.
What to stop, continue and extend.

## Workplace scenario

Use this scenario for discussion, or for assessment if the learner cannot use a case from their
own work.

Nkechi is a learning partner at a software company with 300 staff, most of them remote. Support
engineers take about five months to handle complex tickets alone. New starters complete two
weeks of recorded courses and then ask the same four senior engineers for help in a chat channel.
The senior engineers are behind on their own work, and two have asked to move teams. The support
manager wants a longer induction course. Nkechi has no budget for new content this quarter.

Tasks:

1. Analyse how new support engineers learn now. State the evidence that Nkechi needs.
2. Propose support at the point of work that reduces the load on the senior engineers.
3. State how Nkechi will show the support manager the effect after three months.

## Assessment

Suitable methods: work-based assignment, portfolio of work evidence, project report,
professional discussion, reflective account.

Evidence requirements:

- The evidence comes from the learner's own work with at least one real team or function over a
  period of at least two months.
- It includes the learner's analysis of how the people learn now, one resource or method that the
  learner put into use, and one record of a structured review that the learner led.
- It includes evidence from the work, from before and after, for criterion 4.1.
- Records of real people and incidents are made anonymous.

# Unit bank

Version 0.2.0 (draft).

Every Open Leadership qualification is a set of units from this bank. The rules are in
[credit-and-assessment-rules.md](../credit-and-assessment-rules.md). Each unit has a purpose,
learning outcomes, assessment criteria, indicative content, a workplace scenario and evidence
requirements. The qualifications are in [qualifications/](../../qualifications/README.md).

The bank has 61 units: 19 at Level 3, 28 at Level 5 and 14 at Level 7.

## Shared units

- Management and leadership share three core units in the Level 5 diplomas (OL5-SLF-01,
  OL5-PEO-01, OL5-PEO-02) and two in the Level 7 diplomas (OL7-SLF-01, OL7-STR-01). A learner
  who holds one diploma needs only the units that they do not have for the other.
- The people practice qualifications take their core units on ethics, evidence, culture and
  senior leadership from the shared domains.

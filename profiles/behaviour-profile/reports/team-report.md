# OL Behaviour Profile: team report template

Version 0.1.0 (draft). Text in `{braces}` is replaced for each team.

Rules:

- Each person agreed that their result is part of this report.
- The report shows no summary for a dimension with fewer than five scores.
- A name appears only if that person agreed to it. The default is no names.
- The chart shows one mark for each person. Tell each person this before they agree, because
  colleagues can sometimes work out whose mark is whose.
- Do not label the team as a type. Do not rank people.

The function `team_summary` in the scoring code gives the numbers.

## Read this first

- This report shows the spread of the team on four dimensions of behaviour at work. It is for

## How to read a spread

For each dimension, keep the paragraph that fits.

**Most of the team is higher.** The team does this easily and may do too much of it. Ask what
the team misses because few people take the other position.

**Most of the team is lower.** The same question, in the other direction.

**The team is spread wide.** The team has both positions. This is a resource, and it is also
where friction starts. Ask how the two ends see each other.

**The team is close together in the middle.** The team is flexible here, and nobody may own
this behaviour. Ask who does it when it matters.

## Questions for each dimension

**Voice.** Who speaks first in our meetings, and who speaks last or never? How does a person
with lower Voice get a view heard here? When everybody has a view, who decides?

**Warmth.** How do we notice that a colleague has a problem? Do we give hard messages
plainly? Who does the work of holding the team together, and do we value it?

**Method.** Do we agree what "finished" and "on time" mean? Where do plans help us, and where
do they slow us? What happens when a person with higher Method depends on a person with lower
Method?

**Exploration.** How does a new idea get a fair test here? What do we protect from change,
and why? Do we finish what we start?

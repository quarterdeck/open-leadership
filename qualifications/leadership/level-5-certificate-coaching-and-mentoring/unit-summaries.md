## Core units

### [OL5-COA-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-COA-01.md): Coaching and mentoring practice

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to coach and mentor people at work with a staged process and clear
agreements.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the use of coaching and mentoring at work
2. Plan coaching and mentoring relationships with clear agreements
3. Apply a staged process in coaching and mentoring sessions
4. Evaluate their own coaching and mentoring practice

### [OL5-COA-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-COA-02.md): Building a coaching culture

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan how to make coaching and mentoring a normal part of how an
organisation works.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the present place of coaching and mentoring in an organisation
2. Plan how to extend coaching and mentoring in an organisation
3. Evaluate the effect of coaching and mentoring

### [OL5-COA-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-COA-03.md): Reflective practice for coaches and mentors

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to review their own coaching and mentoring practice and improve it.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the purpose and methods of reflective practice
2. Apply reflective methods to their own sessions
3. Apply feedback and supervision to their practice
4. Plan and evaluate their development as a coach or mentor

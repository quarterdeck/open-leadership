Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. This qualification is not regulated by
Ofqual and is not on the Regulated Qualifications Framework.

## Purpose

This qualification covers two central tasks of a leader at this level, which are to build a team and
to influence people outside it, with one further area that the learner selects.

## Who it is for

A middle manager, a project lead or a professional who leads people and wants focused development
with limited time.

A learner who completes the core units can:

- build a team, develop trust and lead it to sustained results
- analyse stakeholders, and influence and communicate with them to reach an objective

## Entry guidance

- There are no formal entry requirements.
- The demand is that of Level 5: the typical learner is a middle manager or an experienced
  first-line manager. See the [level descriptors](../../../framework/level-descriptors.md).
- The learner needs access to real work that can give evidence for each unit. A learner who is not
  in a suitable role can use a secondment, a project or voluntary work.
- The learner needs English at a level that lets them explain their work in writing and in
  discussion.

## Credit from other learning

- A unit that the learner passed for another Open Leadership qualification counts here. The
  learner does not take it again.
- A centre can accept other evidence of prior learning for up to half of the credit. The centre
  records what it accepted and why.

## Assessment methods

Each unit lists the methods that fit it. The centre selects from them. The methods in this
qualification are: work-based assignment, portfolio of work evidence, observation at work, witness
statement, professional discussion, reflective account, project report and presentation. There are
no examinations. The [assessment guide](assessment-guide.md) gives the method for each unit.

## Grading

- Each unit is pass or refer. To pass a unit, the learner meets every assessment criterion.
- A referred learner submits more evidence for the criteria that are not met.
- The qualification is passed when all units are passed and the panel review is passed.
- There are no grades above pass.

## Evidence requirements

- Evidence comes from the learner's own work. Each unit states what evidence is necessary.
- The learner signs a statement that the evidence is their own.
- A person at work who saw the work, and who is not the assessor, confirms the main evidence.
- Records of real people are made anonymous.
- If the learner used an AI tool, they say where and how.

## Panel review

After the units, the learner presents their work to a panel of two people for about 45 minutes and
answers questions. At least one member comes from outside the learner's team. The rules are in
section 6 of the [credit and assessment rules](../../../framework/credit-and-assessment-rules.md).

## Certificate

Any organisation can issue the certificate if it followed the process in section 6 of the [credit
and assessment rules](../../../framework/credit-and-assessment-rules.md) and keeps the assessment
record. The certificate states the version of this specification and this sentence: "Issued by
[organisation] against the Open Leadership specification. Open Leadership is an independent,
unregulated framework." The holder can use the badge
[`ol-level-5-certificate.svg`](../../../badges/ol-level-5-certificate.svg).

## Progression

- OL Level 5 Diploma in Leadership Practice. The two core units count towards it, and so does each
  optional unit that is in the diploma.

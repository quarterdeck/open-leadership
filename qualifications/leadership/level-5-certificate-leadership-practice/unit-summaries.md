# OL Level 5 Certificate in Leadership Practice

Version 0.2.0 (draft). Documents for this qualification: [specification](specification.md), [unit

This page is a short view. The unit file has the assessment criteria, the indicative content, a
workplace scenario and the evidence requirements.

## Core units

### [OL5-PEO-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-01.md): Building and leading teams

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to build a team, develop trust and lead it to sustained results.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse what a team needs to achieve its purpose
2. Plan the structure and the ways of working of a team
3. Apply leadership practices that develop trust and cooperation
4. Evaluate the results of the team and their own leadership of it

### [OL5-PEO-04](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-04.md): Influence, stakeholders and communication

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to analyse stakeholders, and influence and communicate with them to
reach an objective.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the stakeholders of an objective
2. Select and apply approaches to influence stakeholders
3. Plan and deliver communication to stakeholders
4. Evaluate the result and their own practice

## Optional units

### [OL5-SLF-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-SLF-01.md): Leading yourself: self-awareness and growth

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to evaluate their own strengths, limits and effect on others, and plan
their growth as a leader.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse their own strengths, limits and preferences as a leader
2. Evaluate the effect of their behaviour on other people
3. Plan and manage their growth as a leader

### [OL5-PEO-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-03.md): Leading inclusively

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to create conditions in which different people can contribute fully.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse how far their area of responsibility includes different people
2. Apply fair methods to decisions about people
3. Plan and apply action that lets each person contribute
4. Evaluate the effect of their leadership on inclusion

### [OL5-PEO-05](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-05.md): Wellbeing and sustainable performance

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to manage workload and conditions so that people perform well without
harm to their health.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the effect of work on health and performance in their area
2. Plan workload and the design of work for sustained performance
3. Apply the organisation's procedures to support people whose health is affected
4. Evaluate the effect of their management on wellbeing and results

### [OL5-CHG-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-CHG-01.md): Leading change

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan and lead a change, and bring the people affected with them.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the case for a change and the conditions for it
2. Plan a change
3. Apply methods that bring the people affected with the change
4. Evaluate a change and their own leadership of it

### [OL5-COA-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-COA-01.md): Coaching and mentoring practice

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to coach and mentor people at work with a staged process and clear
agreements.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the use of coaching and mentoring at work
2. Plan coaching and mentoring relationships with clear agreements
3. Apply a staged process in coaching and mentoring sessions
4. Evaluate their own coaching and mentoring practice

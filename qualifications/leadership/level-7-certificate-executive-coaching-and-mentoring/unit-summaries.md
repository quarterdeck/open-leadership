## Core units

### [OL7-COA-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-COA-01.md): Executive coaching and mentoring practice

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to coach and mentor senior people, with clear contracts and
boundaries.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate approaches to the coaching and mentoring of senior people
2. Design contracts and boundaries for work with senior people
3. Lead coaching and mentoring work with senior people
4. Critically evaluate their own practice with senior people

### [OL7-COA-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-COA-02.md): Coaching supervision and ethics

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to use supervision and an ethical framework to maintain the quality of
coaching practice.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the purposes and forms of coaching supervision
2. Formulate an ethical framework for coaching practice
3. Design arrangements that maintain the quality of coaching in an organisation
4. Critically evaluate their own use of supervision and ethics

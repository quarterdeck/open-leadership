# OL Level 3 Award in Management Practice

## Assessment guide

Version 0.2.0 (draft). Documents for this qualification: [specification](specification.md), [unit
summaries](unit-summaries.md), [assessment guide](assessment-guide.md), [centre
guide](centre-guide.md), [learner handbook](learner-handbook.md).

This guide is for assessors, for the person who makes the second check. It applies the [credit and
assessment rules](../../../framework/credit-and-assessment-rules.md) to this qualification. If this
guide and the rules disagree, the rules are correct.

## The standard

This is a Level 3 qualification. The learner works within plans and rules that other people set.
Good evidence shows that the learner completed defined tasks, selected a suitable method, and can
say what worked and what to change. Do not ask for the analysis of theory.

- Judge against the assessment criteria of the unit, and nothing else. Do not add requirements.
- The learner must meet every criterion. A strong answer to one criterion does not make up for a
  missing one.
- Good writing is not the standard. A plain account with strong work evidence passes. A polished
  essay with no work evidence does not.

## Plan the assessment

- Agree an assessment plan with the learner at the start: which work will give evidence, for which
  units, and by when.
- One piece of work can give evidence for more than one unit. Ask the learner to show which
  criteria each item of evidence meets.
- Use the workplace scenario of a unit only if the learner cannot use a case from their own work.
  Record the reason.
- Keep the amount of evidence small. One clear item for each criterion is sufficient.

### How to use each method

| Method | Use it for | Take care that |
|---|---|---|
| Work-based assignment | A task from the learner's job, with a written account of what they did and why | The task is real, and the learner's own part is clear |
| Portfolio of work evidence | Documents that the learner made at work: plans, records, reports, messages | Each item has a note that says which criterion it meets. Personal data is removed |
| Observation at work | Skills that show in action, such as a meeting or a conversation | The people present agree. The observer records what they saw against the criteria |
| Witness statement | Work that the assessor cannot see | The witness saw the work, states facts, and is not the assessor |
| Professional discussion | Reasons, judgement and depth of knowledge | The assessor keeps a record or a recording, with the criteria that were met |
| Reflective account | What the learner learned and what they will change | It refers to real events. General statements are not evidence |
| Project report | A larger piece of work with a start, an end and a result | The scope fits the level and the learner's authority |
| Presentation | Findings and recommendations to an audience | Questions follow, and the answers are recorded |

## Decisions and feedback

- Record a decision for each criterion: met, or not met with the reason.
- The unit result is pass or refer. For a refer, tell the learner which criteria are not met and
  what evidence would meet them.
- Give the result and the feedback in writing. We recommend a reply inside 15 working days.
- The assessor is not the learner's line manager and has no personal interest in the result.

## Authenticity

- The learner signs a statement that the evidence is their own work.
- A person at work who saw the work confirms the main evidence.
- The learner says where and how they used an AI tool. The use of a tool to improve wording is
  acceptable. Evidence that the learner cannot explain is not acceptable.
- If you doubt that the work is the learner's own, hold a professional discussion about it before
  you decide.

## The second check

A second person checks a sample of each assessor's decisions. The second person knows the subject
and did not assess that learner.

- We recommend that the sample is the first three decisions of a new assessor, then one decision
  in five.
- Include pass and refer decisions, and each unit of the qualification during a year.
- If the second person disagrees, the two discuss the evidence against the criteria. If they still
  disagree, a third person decides. Record the result.
- A small organisation can ask a person from another organisation to make the second check.

## The assessment record

Keep this record for each certificate for six years or more:

- the learner's name, the qualification title and the version of the specification
- the units, with the assessor's decision and date for each
- an index of the evidence
- the record of the second check, if the learner was in the sample

Give the learner a copy.

# OL Level 3 Award in Management Practice

## Unit summaries

Version 0.2.0 (draft). Documents for this qualification: [specification](specification.md), [unit
summaries](unit-summaries.md), [assessment guide](assessment-guide.md), [centre
guide](centre-guide.md), [learner handbook](learner-handbook.md).

This page is a short view. The unit file has the assessment criteria, the indicative content, a
workplace scenario and the evidence requirements.

## Core units

### [OL3-OPS-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-OPS-01.md): Planning and organising work

Core unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan, allocate and monitor the work of a team.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan the work of a team for a set period
2. Apply the plan when they allocate work to team members
3. Review progress and adjust the plan

## Optional units

### [OL3-PEO-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PEO-01.md): Leading a team

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to lead a small team so that its members know what to do, work well
together and meet their objectives.

Learning outcomes:

1. Explain the role of a team leader in a small team
2. Plan and direct the daily work of a team
3. Review how well the team works together and how well they lead it

### [OL3-PEO-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PEO-02.md): Managing individual performance

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to set clear objectives, give feedback and deal with good and poor
performance.

Learning outcomes:

1. Set clear objectives and standards with a team member
2. Give feedback and recognise good performance
3. Deal with poor performance within the limits of their role

### [OL3-OPS-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-OPS-02.md): Finding causes and deciding what to do

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to find the cause of a problem at work, compare options and make a
decision.

Learning outcomes:

1. Identify the cause of a problem at work
2. Plan a solution from a comparison of options
3. Carry out a decision and review the result

### [OL3-OPS-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-OPS-03.md): Working with data and digital tools

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to use data and common digital tools to monitor work and report
results.

Learning outcomes:

1. Identify the data that shows how the work of the team is going
2. Use digital tools to organise, analyse and share information
3. Use data to report results and review their practice

### [OL3-OPS-04](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-OPS-04.md): Delivering a small project

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan and deliver a small project on time.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan a small project
2. Carry out the project
3. Review the project

### [OL3-RES-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-RES-01.md): Working with budgets and resources

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to work within a budget and use time, equipment and materials well.

Learning outcomes:

1. Explain how a budget controls the spending of their area
2. Use resources within a budget
3. Review the use of resources in their area

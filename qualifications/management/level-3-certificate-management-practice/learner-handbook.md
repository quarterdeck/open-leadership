# OL Level 3 Certificate in Management Practice

## Learner handbook

Version 0.2.0 (draft). Documents for this qualification: [specification](specification.md), [unit
summaries](unit-summaries.md), [assessment guide](assessment-guide.md), [centre
guide](centre-guide.md), [learner handbook](learner-handbook.md).

## What this qualification is

This qualification covers the full job of a first-line manager: manage yourself, lead the team,
organise the work, and two further areas that fit the learner's role.

It is at Level 3. That is the level of work of a team leader, a supervisor or a new first-line
manager. It has 5 units and 25 credits. One credit is 10 hours, so the total time is about 250
hours. A typical duration is 6 to 10 months.

## What it is not

Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. This qualification is not regulated by
Ofqual, and no awarding organisation or professional body accredits it. Its value comes from the
work that you show and from the open standard that anyone can read. An employer can check exactly
what you did to get it.

## What you study

You complete 3 core units and 2 optional units. Select the optional units with your tutor and your
manager, to fit your job.

If you passed one of these units for another Open Leadership qualification, it counts. You do not
take it again.

## How you spend the time

- Guided learning: about 20 hours for each unit with a tutor, coach or facilitator.
- Private study: reading and thinking.
- Practice at work: you use what you learn in your job. This is the largest part.
- Assessment: you collect and explain your evidence.

## How you are assessed

There are no examinations. You show each learning outcome with evidence from your own work. Each
unit has assessment criteria, and you must meet every one.

- At the start, you agree an assessment plan with your assessor.
- You collect evidence: documents from your work, accounts of what you did, statements from people
  who saw your work, a discussion with your assessor.
- Your assessor decides pass or refer for each unit. Refer means that some criteria are not met
  yet. Your assessor tells you which, and you give more evidence.
- A second person checks some of the assessor's decisions, so that the standard is the same for
  everybody.

### Good evidence

- It is about real work that you did. Say "I", and say what your part was.
- It shows the criterion. Write the number of the criterion next to each item.
- It is short. One clear item for each criterion is sufficient. One item can serve more than one
  criterion.
- It protects other people. Remove names and personal details.
- A person at work who saw the work confirms it.

### AI tools

You can use an AI tool to help you write. Say where and how you used it. The work and the thinking
must be yours. The panel will ask you to explain your work without notes from a tool.

## The panel review

When you have passed all units, you present your work to a panel of two people. It takes about 30
minutes. The model is the oral examination of a research degree, at a smaller scale.

- You speak for about 10 minutes about what you did, why, and what you learned.
- The panel asks questions about your evidence, your decisions and what you would do differently.
- The panel does not test your memory of theory. It wants to hear that the work is yours and that
  you understand it.
- At least one member does not know you from your daily work.
- The result is pass or refer, with written reasons. If you are referred, you can return one time.
- Tell your centre before the day if you need an adjustment.

Questions that you can expect:

- Tell us about one piece of work in your evidence. What did you do, and why did you do it that
  way?
- What did not go as you planned? What did you do then?
- Which rule or procedure of your organisation mattered most here? Why does it exist?
- What do you do differently now?

## Your certificate and badge

- The organisation that assessed you issues your certificate. It states the title, the version and
  the date.
- You get a copy of your assessment record. Keep it. It is the proof behind your certificate.
- You can show the badge
  [`ol-level-3-certificate.svg`](../../../badges/ol-level-3-certificate.svg) on your profile or
  your email signature.
- Write the title in full: "OL Level 3 Certificate in Management Practice". Do not describe it as
  accredited or regulated.

## If something goes wrong

- If you disagree with a decision, ask your centre for a review by a person who took no part in
  it.
- If your job changes and you can no longer get evidence, tell your assessor. You can agree a
  project or another source.
- Units that you passed stay passed. You can stop and continue later.

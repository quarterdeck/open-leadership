## Core units

### [OL5-SLF-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-SLF-01.md): Leading yourself: self-awareness and growth

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to evaluate their own strengths, limits and effect on others, and plan
their growth as a leader.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse their own strengths, limits and preferences as a leader
2. Evaluate the effect of their behaviour on other people
3. Plan and manage their growth as a leader

### [OL5-PEO-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-01.md): Building and leading teams

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to build a team, develop trust and lead it to sustained results.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse what a team needs to achieve its purpose
2. Plan the structure and the ways of working of a team
3. Apply leadership practices that develop trust and cooperation
4. Evaluate the results of the team and their own leadership of it

### [OL5-PEO-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-02.md): Managing performance and developing people

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to manage the performance of a team and develop the capability of its
members.

Learning outcomes:

1. Set objectives and standards that connect the work of a team to the aims of the organisation
2. Review performance and give feedback that changes it
3. Develop the capability of team members
4. Evaluate their own practice in the management of performance

### [OL5-OPS-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-OPS-01.md): Operational planning and delivery

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to turn objectives into an operational plan and manage its delivery.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the objectives and the demand that an operational plan must meet
2. Plan the operations of their area for a period
3. Apply the plan and control its delivery
4. Evaluate the delivery of the plan and their own practice

### [OL5-OPS-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-OPS-03.md): Decisions from evidence and data

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to collect, analyse and interpret evidence to make and justify a
management decision.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan the evidence that a management decision needs
2. Analyse evidence and interpret the findings
3. Justify a decision from the evidence
4. Evaluate a decision and their own use of evidence

### [OL5-RES-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-RES-01.md): Managing budgets and financial performance

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to prepare a budget, monitor financial performance and act on
variances.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the finances of their area of responsibility
2. Plan a budget
3. Apply financial control during the budget period
4. Evaluate the financial performance of their area and their own practice

## Optional units

### [OL5-OPS-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-OPS-02.md): Managing projects

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan, lead and close a project with a defined scope, budget and
risk.

Learning outcomes:

1. Justify a project and its scope
2. Plan the schedule, budget, risks and roles of a project
3. Apply controls that keep a project within its scope, time and budget
4. Evaluate a project and their leadership of it

### [OL5-OPS-04](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-OPS-04.md): Quality and continuous improvement

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to measure quality and lead an improvement to a process or a service.

Learning outcomes:

1. Assess the quality of a process or a service
2. Analyse a process to find the causes of poor quality
3. Plan and apply an improvement
4. Evaluate how an improvement is kept and how they lead improvement

### [OL5-RES-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-RES-02.md): Managing risk

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to identify, assess and control risks in their area of responsibility.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the risks that their area of responsibility faces
2. Assess and rank risks
3. Plan and apply controls
4. Evaluate the control of risk in their area

### [OL5-CHG-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-CHG-01.md): Leading change

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan and lead a change, and bring the people affected with them.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the case for a change and the conditions for it
2. Plan a change
3. Apply methods that bring the people affected with the change
4. Evaluate a change and their own leadership of it

### [OL5-CHG-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-CHG-02.md): Innovation in practice

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to develop and test a new idea that improves a product, a service or a
way of working.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse opportunities for innovation
2. Plan and apply a test of a new idea
3. Evaluate a test and justify a recommendation
4. Evaluate the conditions for innovation in their team

### [OL5-STR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-STR-01.md): Organisations, culture and context

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to analyse how the structure, culture and external context of an
organisation affect its performance.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse how the structure of an organisation affects its performance
2. Analyse the culture of an organisation and its effects
3. Analyse the external context of an organisation
4. Apply the analysis to their own area of responsibility

### [OL5-STR-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-STR-02.md): Contributing to strategy

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to contribute analysis and proposals to the strategy of an
organisation and align a team with it.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the strategy of the organisation and the place of their area in it
2. Analyse evidence and options for a strategic proposal
3. Justify a proposal to the people who decide strategy
4. Plan the work of a team to carry out the strategy

### [OL5-PEO-04](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-04.md): Influence, stakeholders and communication

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to analyse stakeholders, and influence and communicate with them to
reach an objective.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the stakeholders of an objective
2. Select and apply approaches to influence stakeholders
3. Plan and deliver communication to stakeholders
4. Evaluate the result and their own practice

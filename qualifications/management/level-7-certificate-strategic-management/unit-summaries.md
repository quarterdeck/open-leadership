## Core units

### [OL7-STR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-STR-01.md): Strategy development and execution

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to develop a strategy from evidence and lead its execution.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the strategic position of an organisation
2. Formulate a strategy from evidence
3. Design and lead the execution of a strategy
4. Lead the review and adaptation of a strategy

## Optional units

### [OL7-OPS-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-OPS-01.md): Organisational performance and operating models

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to evaluate how an organisation creates value and redesign how it
operates.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate how an organisation creates value
2. Design the way in which an organisation measures and governs its performance
3. Design a change to an operating model
4. Lead a change to an operating model and appraise its results

### [OL7-RES-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-RES-01.md): Strategic finance and risk

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to use financial information and risk analysis to make strategic
decisions.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the financial position and performance of an organisation
2. Appraise a strategic investment
3. Design the way in which an organisation governs strategic risk
4. Lead a strategic decision on finance and risk

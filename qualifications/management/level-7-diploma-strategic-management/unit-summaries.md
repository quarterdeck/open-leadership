## Core units

### [OL7-SLF-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-SLF-01.md): Leading at senior level: self, ethics and impact

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to evaluate their own leadership critically and act ethically under
pressure and scrutiny.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate their own leadership and its effect on the organisation
2. Formulate ethical positions on decisions in which obligations compete
3. Lead with integrity under pressure and scrutiny
4. Design their own development for long-term effect as a senior leader

### [OL7-STR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-STR-01.md): Strategy development and execution

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to develop a strategy from evidence and lead its execution.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the strategic position of an organisation
2. Formulate a strategy from evidence
3. Design and lead the execution of a strategy
4. Lead the review and adaptation of a strategy

### [OL7-OPS-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-OPS-01.md): Organisational performance and operating models

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to evaluate how an organisation creates value and redesign how it
operates.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate how an organisation creates value
2. Design the way in which an organisation measures and governs its performance
3. Design a change to an operating model
4. Lead a change to an operating model and appraise its results

## Optional units

### [OL7-RES-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-RES-01.md): Strategic finance and risk

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to use financial information and risk analysis to make strategic
decisions.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the financial position and performance of an organisation
2. Appraise a strategic investment
3. Design the way in which an organisation governs strategic risk
4. Lead a strategic decision on finance and risk

### [OL7-CHG-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-CHG-01.md): Leading strategic change and innovation

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to lead change and innovation across an organisation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the need and the capacity for strategic change in an organisation
2. Formulate a strategy for change across an organisation
3. Design the conditions for innovation in an organisation
4. Lead strategic change and critically evaluate their own leadership of it

### [OL7-STR-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-STR-02.md): Governance, stakeholders and the external environment

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to work with boards, regulators, partners and the public, and respond
to the external environment.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the governance of an organisation
2. Lead relationships with stakeholders whose interests compete
3. Formulate a response to the external environment
4. Lead the public accountability of an organisation

### [OL7-STR-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-STR-03.md): Applied investigation

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to design and complete an investigation into a strategic question at
work, and act on the findings.

Learning outcomes:

1. Formulate a strategic question for investigation
2. Design an investigation that is sound and ethical
3. Synthesise evidence into findings
4. Formulate recommendations and lead action on them

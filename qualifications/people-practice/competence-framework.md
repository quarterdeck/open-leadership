# People practice competence framework

Version 0.2.0 (draft).

This framework says what a people practitioner does, in eight practice areas and at three depths.
"People practice" is the work of human resources (HR) and of learning and development (L&D).

Use it to:

- select units and plan development
- write an account of experience for the [recognition scheme](recognition-scheme.md)
- describe a job or a team

Open Leadership wrote this framework. The practice areas were chosen with reference to public
sources under the Open Government Licence: the HR Career Framework of the UK Civil Service and
the Level 5 apprenticeship standard for HR. See [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md). It is an
independent framework, and no professional body endorses it.

## The three depths

| Depth | The practitioner | Typical level of unit |
|---|---|---|
| Support | Carries out defined tasks, follows procedures, keeps accurate records and knows when to refer a matter to another person | 3 |
| Practise | Advises managers, runs cases and projects without close supervision, and makes decisions inside policy | 5 |
| Lead | Sets policy and strategy, leads other practitioners, and answers to senior leaders for the results | 7 |

A practitioner is often at different depths in different areas. That is normal.

## The eight practice areas

Areas 1 to 3 apply to every practitioner. Areas 4 to 8 are specialist areas.

### 1. Professional judgement

Act ethically, keep confidences, and stay inside the limits of your competence.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Follows the rules on confidentiality and fair treatment. Says when a request seems wrong, and refers it |
| Practise | Gives advice that weighs the interests of the person, the manager and the organisation. Refuses to act against the law or against fair treatment, and explains why |
| Lead | Sets the ethical standard for the function. Challenges senior leaders when a decision would harm people or the organisation |

Units: OL3-SLF-01, OL5-SLF-02, OL7-SLF-01.

### 2. Evidence and people data

Collect, protect and use information about people and work.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Keeps people records accurate, secure and lawful. Produces standard reports |
| Practise | Frames a question, collects data and other evidence, and uses the analysis to advise a decision |
| Lead | Decides what the organisation measures about its people. Uses evidence to test strategy, and investigates questions that have no ready answer |

Units: OL3-PPR-04, OL3-OPS-03, OL5-OPS-03, OL5-PPR-08, OL7-STR-03.

### 3. The organisation and its people

Know how the organisation works, what it needs from its people, and what they need from it.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Explains what the people function does at each stage of employment, and who its customers are |
| Practise | Analyses the culture and context of the organisation, and fits people practice to them |
| Lead | Writes the people strategy from the strategy of the organisation, and plans the workforce that it needs |

Units: OL3-PPR-01, OL3-STR-01, OL5-STR-01, OL7-PPR-01, OL7-STR-02.

### 4. Resourcing

Find, select and keep the people that the organisation needs.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Runs the steps of recruitment and onboarding fairly and on time |
| Practise | Plans resourcing for a part of the organisation. Selects methods of selection that are fair and that predict performance. Manages talent and succession |
| Lead | Plans the workforce for the long term, and decides when to build, buy or borrow skills |

Units: OL3-PPR-02, OL5-PPR-01, OL7-PPR-01.

### 5. Employment relations

Keep the relationship between the organisation and its people fair, lawful and productive.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Gives first-line information on policy. Keeps the records of a case. Refers a case when it goes beyond policy |
| Practise | Manages discipline, grievance, absence and capability cases. Applies employment law. Works with employee representatives |
| Lead | Sets the approach to employee voice, to trade unions and to conflict. Manages legal and reputational risk at the level of the organisation |

Units: OL3-PEO-04, OL5-PPR-02, OL5-PPR-07, OL7-PPR-02.

### 6. Reward

Pay and recognise people in a way that is fair, lawful and affordable.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Administers pay and benefits changes accurately. Answers standard questions |
| Practise | Evaluates jobs, compares pay with the market, runs pay reviews and analyses pay gaps |
| Lead | Sets reward strategy and policy, and answers to senior leaders for its cost and its fairness |

Units: OL5-PPR-03, OL7-PPR-02.

### 7. Learning and development

Help people and the organisation to learn.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Organises learning, supports learners and records the results |
| Practise | Finds learning needs, designs learning, facilitates it, supports learning during work, and evaluates the effect |
| Lead | Sets the learning strategy. Develops leaders. Shows the value of learning to the organisation |

Units: OL3-PPR-03, OL3-COA-01, OL5-PPR-04, OL5-PPR-05, OL5-PPR-06, OL5-COA-01, OL5-COA-02,
OL7-PPR-04.

### 8. Organisation development, inclusion and wellbeing

Shape the structure, the culture and the conditions of work so that people can do good work.

| Depth | What the practitioner does |
|---|---|
| Support | Supports wellbeing and inclusion activity, and points people to help |
| Practise | Leads a change in a part of the organisation. Finds and removes barriers to inclusion. Plans for sustainable performance |
| Lead | Designs the organisation and leads the development of its culture. Leads strategic change |

Units: OL3-PEO-05, OL3-CHG-01, OL5-PEO-03, OL5-PEO-05, OL5-CHG-01, OL7-PPR-03, OL7-CHG-01,
OL7-PEO-01.

## How the framework connects to the qualifications

| Qualification | Depth | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| OL Level 3 Certificate in People Practice | Support | Areas 1 to 3, and two specialist areas through the optional units |
| OL Level 5 Diploma in People Practice (people management pathway) | Practise | Areas 1 to 6 |
| OL Level 5 Diploma in People Practice (learning and development pathway) | Practise | Areas 1 to 3 and area 7 |
| OL Level 7 Diploma in Strategic People Practice | Lead | Areas 1 to 3, and three specialist areas through the optional units |

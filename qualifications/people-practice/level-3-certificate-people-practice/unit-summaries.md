## Core units

### [OL3-SLF-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-SLF-01.md): Managing yourself and your development

Core unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to manage their own time, priorities and conduct, and plan their
development as a manager.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan their own time and priorities to meet the needs of the team
2. Apply the standards of conduct that the organisation expects of a manager
3. Plan and review their own development as a manager

### [OL3-PPR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PPR-01.md): The people function and the employee life cycle

Core unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to describe the work of a people function from recruitment to exit,
and their own role in it.

Learning outcomes:

1. Describe the work of a people function and how it supports the organisation
2. Explain the stages of the employee life cycle and carry out people tasks in them
3. Review their own role in the people function

### [OL3-PPR-04](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PPR-04.md): People records, data and the law

Core unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to keep accurate people records and apply the main rules of employment
law and data protection.

Learning outcomes:

1. Explain the main rules of UK employment law that apply to daily people work
2. Apply UK data protection law to people records
3. Keep accurate people records and review their own practice

## Optional units

### [OL3-PPR-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PPR-02.md): Recruitment and onboarding in practice

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to support a fair recruitment process and the first weeks of a new
employee.

Learning outcomes:

1. Explain what makes a recruitment process fair and lawful
2. Carry out the practical tasks of a recruitment process
3. Plan and review the first weeks of a new employee

### [OL3-PPR-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PPR-03.md): Supporting learning at work

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to find a learning need and support a learning activity for a person
or a group.

Learning outcomes:

1. Identify a learning need of a person or a group
2. Plan and support a learning activity that meets the need
3. Review the learning activity and their own part in it

### [OL3-PEO-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PEO-03.md): Communicating at work

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to communicate clearly with a team, with managers and with customers,
in speech and in writing.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan communication that fits its purpose and its audience
2. Communicate in speech with a team, with managers and with customers
3. Communicate in writing and review their communication

### [OL3-PEO-05](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-PEO-05.md): Supporting wellbeing and inclusion

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to support the wellbeing of team members and treat people fairly.

Learning outcomes:

1. Explain the part of a team leader in wellbeing and fair treatment
2. Support the wellbeing of team members
3. Treat team members fairly and include each of them

### [OL3-OPS-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-OPS-03.md): Working with data and digital tools

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to use data and common digital tools to monitor work and report
results.

Learning outcomes:

1. Identify the data that shows how the work of the team is going
2. Use digital tools to organise, analyse and share information
3. Use data to report results and review their practice

### [OL3-STR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL3-STR-01.md): Understanding your organisation

Optional unit. 5 credits. 50 hours.

This unit enables the learner to explain the purpose, structure and customers of their organisation
and how their team contributes.

Learning outcomes:

1. Describe the purpose, structure and customers of their organisation
2. Explain how the world outside the organisation affects it
3. Explain and review the contribution of their team

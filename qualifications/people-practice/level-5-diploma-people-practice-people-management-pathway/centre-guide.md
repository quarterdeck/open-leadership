# OL Level 5 Diploma in People Practice (people management pathway)

## Centre guide

Version 0.2.0 (draft). Documents for this qualification: [specification](specification.md), [unit
summaries](unit-summaries.md), [assessment guide](assessment-guide.md), [centre
guide](centre-guide.md), [learner handbook](learner-handbook.md).

A centre is an organisation that delivers or assesses this qualification: an employer, a training
provider, a college, a charity or a group of practitioners. You do not need approval, and there is
no fee. You are responsible for the quality of what you do and for the truth of what you say about
it.

Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. This qualification is not regulated by
Ofqual and is not on the Regulated Qualifications Framework.

## The people you need

- A tutor, coach or facilitator who guides the learning.
- An assessor who knows the subject and holds, or works at, Level 5 or above. The assessor is not
  the learner's line manager.
- A second person who checks a sample of the assessor's decisions.
- A panel of three people for each learner at the end. The chair has not taught or assessed the
  learner. One member comes from outside the learner's team.
- One person can have more than one of these roles for different learners. A small organisation
  can share assessors, second checkers and panel members with another organisation.

## Time

| | |
|---|---|
| Total qualification time | 420 hours |
| Guide for guided learning | 147 hours |
| Typical duration | 10 to 16 months, at 6 to 10 hours each week |

Total time includes guided learning, private study, practice at work and assessment. Much of it is
work that the learner does in their job. The guide for guided learning is a guide. Use more or less
if the learner can still meet the outcomes.

## A delivery plan

This plan is an example. Change the order to fit the learner's work.

| Step | Unit | Guided learning | Suggested form |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start | 2 hours | Explain the qualification. Agree the assessment plan and the optional unit |
| 2 | OL5-SLF-02 Ethics and professional judgement | 21 hours | Workshops or coaching sessions, practice at work between them, then assessment |
| 3 | OL5-OPS-03 Decisions from evidence and data | 21 hours | Workshops or coaching sessions, practice at work between them, then assessment |
| 4 | OL5-STR-01 Organisations, culture and context | 21 hours | Workshops or coaching sessions, practice at work between them, then assessment |
| 5 | OL5-PPR-01 Recruitment, retention and succession | 21 hours | Workshops or coaching sessions, practice at work between them, then assessment |
| 6 | OL5-PPR-02 Employee relations | 21 hours | Workshops or coaching sessions, practice at work between them, then assessment |
| 7 | OL5-PPR-03 Reward and recognition | 21 hours | Workshops or coaching sessions, practice at work between them, then assessment |
| 8 | Optional unit (for example OL5-PPR-07 Employment law in practice) | 21 hours | Workshops or coaching sessions, practice at work between them, then assessment |
| 9 | Panel review | 45 minutes for each learner | See the assessment guide |

- Units can run one after the other or together. Start each unit with the work that will give the
  evidence.
- Guided learning can be in a room or online, in a group or one to one.
- Use the workplace scenario of each unit for discussion.
- All Open Leadership material is free to copy and adapt. You can add your own material.

## Support for learners

- Check at the start that the learner's work can give the evidence. If it cannot, agree a project,
  a secondment or another source with the learner's manager.
- Ask the learner's manager to give time for the work and to be, or to name, a witness.
- Make reasonable adjustments so that a disabled learner is not at a disadvantage. Change the
  method of assessment, never the criteria. For example, replace a written account with a recorded
  discussion.
- Give each learner the [learner handbook](learner-handbook.md).

## Assessment and quality

Follow the [assessment guide](assessment-guide.md). In short:

- an assessor judges each unit against its criteria
- a second person checks a sample
- a panel of three reviews each learner at the end
- you keep the assessment record for six years, then delete it

We recommend a simple appeal route: a learner who disagrees with a decision can ask for a review by
a person who took no part in it. Tell learners about the route at the start.

## Records and personal data

- The assessment record contains personal data. You are the controller of that data under UK data
  protection law.
- Tell the learner what you keep, why, and for how long.
- Keep an index of the evidence. The evidence itself can stay with the learner or the employer.
- Show the record to a person who asks to verify a certificate only with the learner's permission.

## Certificates, badges and what you can say

- Issue the certificate in your own name. It states the learner's name, the full title "OL Level 5
  Diploma in People Practice (people management pathway)", the version of the specification, the
  date, and this sentence: "Issued by [organisation] against the Open Leadership specification.
  Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework."
- The learner and your organisation can use the badge
  [`ol-level-5-diploma.svg`](../../../badges/ol-level-5-diploma.svg) with reference to that
  learner or that programme. Do not change the words on it.
- You can say that your programme "leads to" or "is assessed against" this qualification.
- Do not say or imply that the qualification, your programme or your organisation is accredited,
  regulated, endorsed or approved by a regulator, an awarding organisation, a professional body or
  Quarterdeck.

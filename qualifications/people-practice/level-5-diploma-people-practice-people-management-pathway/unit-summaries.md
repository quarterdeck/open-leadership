## Core units

### [OL5-SLF-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-SLF-02.md): Ethics and professional judgement

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to apply ethical principles to decisions at work and act with
professional integrity.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the ethical content of decisions at work
2. Apply professional judgement to a dilemma that the rules do not settle
3. Apply the practices that protect integrity and trust
4. Evaluate their own ethical practice

### [OL5-OPS-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-OPS-03.md): Decisions from evidence and data

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to collect, analyse and interpret evidence to make and justify a
management decision.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan the evidence that a management decision needs
2. Analyse evidence and interpret the findings
3. Justify a decision from the evidence
4. Evaluate a decision and their own use of evidence

### [OL5-STR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-STR-01.md): Organisations, culture and context

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to analyse how the structure, culture and external context of an
organisation affect its performance.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse how the structure of an organisation affects its performance
2. Analyse the culture of an organisation and its effects
3. Analyse the external context of an organisation
4. Apply the analysis to their own area of responsibility

### [OL5-PPR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PPR-01.md): Resourcing and talent

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan for the people an organisation needs, and attract, select and
keep them.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan for the people and skills that a part of the organisation needs
2. Plan attraction and selection that find capable people fairly
3. Plan action to keep and move talent in the organisation
4. Evaluate resourcing practice and their own part in it

### [OL5-PPR-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PPR-02.md): Employee relations

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to manage the relationship between an organisation and its employees,
which includes discipline, grievance and consultation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the employment relationship in an organisation
2. Manage discipline and grievance cases fairly
3. Plan and support consultation on a change that affects employees
4. Evaluate their own practice in employee relations

### [OL5-PPR-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PPR-03.md): Reward and recognition

Core unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to evaluate and apply approaches to pay, benefits and recognition.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse how reward supports the aims of an organisation
2. Evaluate approaches to pay and benefits
3. Plan a change to reward or recognition practice
4. Evaluate their own practice in reward

## Optional units

### [OL5-PPR-07](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PPR-07.md): Employment law in practice

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to apply employment law to common situations and know when to get
specialist advice.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse how UK employment law shapes the relationship between an organisation and the people
   who work for it
2. Apply employment law to common situations during employment
3. Apply employment law to the end of employment
4. Evaluate whether their advice is current and within the limits of their competence

### [OL5-PPR-08](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PPR-08.md): People analytics

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to use people data to answer a question that matters to the
organisation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Plan an analysis of people data that answers a question from the organisation
2. Apply legal and ethical standards to the use of people data
3. Analyse people data
4. Present findings that lead to action, and evaluate their own practice

### [OL5-PPR-04](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PPR-04.md): Designing learning

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to analyse a learning need and design a learning programme to meet it.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse a learning need and the people who have it
2. Design a learning programme that meets the need
3. Plan the delivery and the evaluation of the programme
4. Evaluate a learning design and their own design practice

### [OL5-PEO-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-03.md): Leading inclusively

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to create conditions in which different people can contribute fully.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse how far their area of responsibility includes different people
2. Apply fair methods to decisions about people
3. Plan and apply action that lets each person contribute
4. Evaluate the effect of their leadership on inclusion

### [OL5-PEO-05](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-PEO-05.md): Wellbeing and sustainable performance

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to manage workload and conditions so that people perform well without
harm to their health.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the effect of work on health and performance in their area
2. Plan workload and the design of work for sustained performance
3. Apply the organisation's procedures to support people whose health is affected
4. Evaluate the effect of their management on wellbeing and results

### [OL5-CHG-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-CHG-01.md): Leading change

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to plan and lead a change, and bring the people affected with them.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the case for a change and the conditions for it
2. Plan a change
3. Apply methods that bring the people affected with the change
4. Evaluate a change and their own leadership of it

### [OL5-COA-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL5-COA-01.md): Coaching and mentoring practice

Optional unit. 6 credits. 60 hours.

This unit enables the learner to coach and mentor people at work with a recognised process and clear
agreements.

Learning outcomes:

1. Analyse the use of coaching and mentoring at work
2. Plan coaching and mentoring relationships with clear agreements
3. Apply a recognised process in coaching and mentoring sessions
4. Evaluate their own coaching and mentoring practice

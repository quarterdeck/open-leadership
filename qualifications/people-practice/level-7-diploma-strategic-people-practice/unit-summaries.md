## Core units

### [OL7-SLF-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-SLF-01.md): Leading at senior level: self, ethics and impact

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to evaluate their own leadership critically and act ethically under
pressure and scrutiny.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate their own leadership and its effect on the organisation
2. Formulate ethical positions on decisions in which obligations compete
3. Lead with integrity under pressure and scrutiny
4. Design their own development for long-term effect as a senior leader

### [OL7-PPR-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-PPR-01.md): People strategy and workforce planning

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to develop a people strategy and a workforce plan that support the
strategy of the organisation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate what the strategy and the environment of an organisation demand of its
   workforce
2. Formulate a people strategy that supports the strategy of the organisation
3. Design a workforce plan for an organisation or a large part of one
4. Lead the adoption of the strategy and appraise its results

### [OL7-STR-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-STR-03.md): Applied investigation

Core unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to design and complete an investigation into a strategic question at
work, and act on the findings.

Learning outcomes:

1. Formulate a strategic question for investigation
2. Design an investigation that is sound and ethical
3. Synthesise evidence into findings
4. Formulate recommendations and lead action on them

## Optional units

### [OL7-PPR-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-PPR-02.md): Strategic employment relations and reward

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to set the direction for employment relations and reward across an
organisation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the state of employment relations and reward in an organisation
2. Formulate the direction for employment relations across an organisation
3. Design the direction for reward across an organisation
4. Lead a major change in employment relations or reward and appraise its results

### [OL7-PPR-03](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-PPR-03.md): Organisation design and development

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to design structures and lead development work that improve how an
organisation performs.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate how the present organisation helps or limits performance
2. Design a structure for an organisation or a large part of one
3. Lead development work that improves how people work together
4. Lead the transition to a new design and appraise the results

### [OL7-PPR-04](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-PPR-04.md): Strategic learning and leadership development

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to set the direction for learning and for the development of leaders
across an organisation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the capability that an organisation needs and how well it builds that
   capability
2. Formulate a learning strategy for an organisation
3. Design how an organisation develops its leaders
4. Lead the strategy and appraise its value to the organisation

### [OL7-PEO-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-PEO-01.md): Leading leaders and shaping culture

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to develop other leaders and shape the culture of an organisation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the culture of an organisation and the forces that sustain it
2. Formulate and lead action to shape the culture that the strategy needs
3. Lead and develop the leaders who report to them
4. Critically evaluate their own effect on leaders and on culture

### [OL7-CHG-01](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-CHG-01.md): Leading strategic change and innovation

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to lead change and innovation across an organisation.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the need and the capacity for strategic change in an organisation
2. Formulate a strategy for change across an organisation
3. Design the conditions for innovation in an organisation
4. Lead strategic change and critically evaluate their own leadership of it

### [OL7-STR-02](../../../framework/unit-bank/OL7-STR-02.md): Governance, stakeholders and the external environment

Optional unit. 10 credits. 100 hours.

This unit enables the learner to work with boards, regulators, partners and the public, and respond
to the external environment.

Learning outcomes:

1. Critically evaluate the governance of an organisation
2. Lead relationships with stakeholders whose interests compete
3. Formulate a response to the external environment
4. Lead the public accountability of an organisation

# People practice recognition scheme

Version 0.2.0 (draft). This scheme is a first design. It is open for change.

A qualification shows what a person learned. A recognition grade shows what a person can do in
practice: knowledge and experience together. The scheme has three grades.

Open Leadership is an independent, unregulated framework. A grade is not membership of a
professional body, and it is not a chartered title. No body endorses it.

## The grades

| Grade | Depth | Knowledge | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| OL Associate in People Practice | Support | OL Level 3 Certificate in People Practice, or a comparable qualification | 1 year in a people practice role |
| OL Practitioner in People Practice | Practise | OL Level 5 Diploma in People Practice, or a comparable qualification | 2 years, in which the person advised managers or ran cases or projects without close supervision |
| OL Senior Practitioner in People Practice | Lead | OL Level 7 Diploma in Strategic People Practice, or a comparable qualification | 4 years, with 1 year or more in which the person set policy or strategy or led other practitioners |

The depths are those of the [competence framework](competence-framework.md).

- "A comparable qualification" is a qualification in HR or L&D at the same level or above, from
  any awarding organisation or university. The panel records which qualification it accepted.
- Experience can be employed, self-employed or voluntary. Part-time experience counts in
  proportion.

### The route for experience only

A person with no qualification can get a grade. They need two more years of experience than the
table states, and their account covers each of the three general practice areas of the
competence framework (areas 1 to 3) and two or more specialist areas.

## Who confirms a grade

The same rule applies as for certificates: any organisation can confirm a grade if it follows
this process and keeps the record. Nobody needs approval from Quarterdeck or from any other
body. An employer, a provider or a group of practitioners can operate the scheme.

### Step 1: the account of practice

The candidate writes an account of their practice.

| Grade | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Associate | About 1,000 words | Two examples of work at the Support depth, in two practice areas |
| Practitioner | About 2,000 words | Three examples at the Practise depth, in three practice areas. One example shows a difficult judgement |
| Senior Practitioner | About 3,000 words | Three examples at the Lead depth, in three practice areas. One example shows a change in the organisation that the candidate led, with its results |

Each example states the situation, what the candidate did, why, and the result. A person who saw
the work, such as a manager or a client, confirms each example in writing. Records of real
people are made anonymous.

### Step 2: the review

| Grade | Review |
|---|---|
| Associate | One reviewer reads the account and the confirmations. A second person checks a sample of the reviewer's decisions |
| Practitioner | A professional discussion of about 45 minutes with a panel of two people |
| Senior Practitioner | A professional discussion of about 60 minutes with a panel of three people. One member or more comes from outside the candidate's organisation |

- A reviewer or a panel member practises at the depth of the grade or above, and has no personal
  interest in the result.
- The chair of a panel has not managed or taught the candidate.
- The result is confirmed or not yet confirmed, with written reasons. A candidate who is not
  yet confirmed can apply again after six months.

### Step 3: the record

The organisation keeps a record for six years or more: the candidate's name, the grade, the
qualification that was accepted or the use of the experience route, the account, the
confirmations, the names and roles of the reviewers, their statements of independence, the
decision, the reasons and the date. The candidate gets a copy.

## Keep a grade current

A grade states its date. To describe a grade as current, the holder keeps a record of continuing
professional development: 20 hours or more in each year, with a short note on what changed in
their practice. Nobody approves the record. The holder shows it on request. The rules for
Open Leadership CPD are in section 8 of the
[credit and assessment rules](../../framework/credit-and-assessment-rules.md).

## What a holder can say

- "OL Associate in People Practice (2027), confirmed by [organisation] against the Open
  Leadership recognition scheme."
- Do not shorten a grade to letters after a name.
- Do not say or imply that the grade is membership of a professional body, a chartered title, a
  licence to practise, or accredited or regulated.

## Open questions

- No organisation operates the scheme for the public yet. Until one does, a person needs an
  employer, a provider or a group of practitioners that follows this process.
- The scheme has no badges yet.

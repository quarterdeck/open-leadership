# Unit overlap check

Date: 18 November 2026. Unit bank version 0.2.0.

The specification asks for a check of overlap between units after the three qualification
families are written. This page records the method and the result.

## Method

1. Each writer listed the units whose scope is close to another unit.
2. A script compared each of the 695 assessment criteria with each criterion of the other
   units. It measured the share of words that two criteria have in common, without common
   words such as "the" and "of".

## Result

No two units assess the same thing. 99 pairs of criteria share half of their words or more.
They are of three kinds.

| Kind | Example | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| The review of own practice | "Plan changes to their practice, with reasons" is in six Level 5 units | Intended. Each unit ends with a review of the learner's own practice in that subject |
| The same skill at two levels | OL3-PEO-02 and OL5-PEO-02 both agree objectives. The Level 3 unit is about one team member. The Level 5 unit is about a team, its measures and the link to the organisation | Intended. It shows progression |
| The same skill in two contexts | OL3-COA-01 and OL3-PEO-02 both give feedback that describes the work and its effect. OL7-SLF-01 and OL7-CHG-01 both appraise effects on people, money and reputation | Accepted. One piece of evidence can serve both units |

## Boundaries to keep

When a person changes one of these units, they must read the other.

| Units | Boundary |
|---|---|
| OL5-PPR-02 and OL5-PPR-07 | OL5-PPR-02 is the conduct of cases and consultation. OL5-PPR-07 is the legal tests and the advice |
| OL3-PPR-04 and OL5-PPR-07 | The Level 3 unit is records, data protection and an introduction to the areas of law. The Level 5 unit applies law to cases |
| OL5-OPS-03, OL5-PPR-08 and OL7-STR-03 | OL5-OPS-03 is the general method for evidence. OL5-PPR-08 adds people data, its law and its ethics. OL7-STR-03 is a full investigation with a sponsor |
| OL5-PPR-01 and OL7-PPR-01 | Workforce planning for a team or a function, and for the whole organisation |
| OL7-PEO-01 and OL7-PPR-04 | The leader's own development of their leaders, and the system of leadership development |
| OL7-PPR-03, OL7-OPS-01 and OL7-CHG-01 | Structure and roles; the operating model and performance; the change programme |
| OL7-STR-01, OL7-STR-02 and OL7-RES-01 | Position, choice and execution; governance and the external environment; finance and risk |
| OL5-OPS-04, OL5-CHG-02 and OL3-CHG-01 | The quality of a process; a new idea and its test; support for a change that others lead |
| OL5-OPS-02 and OL5-RES-02 | The risks of one project; the continuing risks of an area |
| OL5-PEO-04, OL5-CHG-01 and OL5-STR-02 | Influence for one objective; a change; a proposal inside the planning process |
| OL3-PEO-05, OL5-PEO-03 and OL5-PEO-05 | One Level 3 unit covers wellbeing and inclusion. Two Level 5 units divide them |
| OL3-COA-01 and OL3-COA-02 | The skills of one conversation; an agreed series of conversations with records |

## Known faults

- Some first names appear in more than one workplace scenario (for example Callum, Priya and
  Tomasz). The scenarios are different. A later version can change the names.
- Several coaching units describe the stages of a coaching conversation in plain words. The
  originality review (Phase 5) must check that the wording is not close to a published model.
